# Use your session list as an inbox

**Icon:** tray

**Kind:** New

Archive a session that waits on someone, and the Kit brings it back when the wait ends.

- Archive the session when it says "You can archive this session."
- When the reply comes, the session is back in your list and the Kit sends you a message.
- To set how the Kit sends the message, use **Reach me by** in **Settings**.
