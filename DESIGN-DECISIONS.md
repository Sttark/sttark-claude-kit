# Design decisions

Where Sttark Claude Kit departs from the Sttark Brand and App Guidelines' Apps chapters: the native Mac app (the guide is written for web apps), and its phone pages, the web app in `phone/` at kit.sttark.com. Each entry: the part, the rule, the decision and why.

| Part | Rule (chapter, section) | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The window and the What's new popup | Look, Type in apps (body 16 px, buttons 15 px Semibold) | Body 13 pt, small text 12 pt, titles 15 to 19 pt, Helvetica Neue at 400 and 600 | A Mac window at web sizes reads as oversized next to every other Mac app; these are macOS's own sizes. Nothing is under 12. |
| The window | Look, Buttons (40 px tall, ink outline) and Forms (44 px fields) | macOS's own push buttons, switches, checkboxes and pop-up menus | People know them, they follow the system's light and dark look, and they work with the keyboard and VoiceOver. The one main action, Got it in the popup, keeps the green fill. |
| The window | Look, Color rules (a chosen option in ink) | Effort levels are an ink segment; switches use the system accent color | An ink switch is near white in dark mode and can't be read; the system color is clear in both. |
| The window | Look, Top bar | No black top bar; a sidebar with Sessions, Settings and What's new | A Mac window has its own title bar; the sidebar is the native way between a window's pages, and it shows the current page with the green "you are here" wash. |
| The window | Look, Icons (Lucide) | SF Symbols | The system's own icons match the system's controls at every size. |
| The window | Look, Dark mode (a light, auto, dark switch) | It follows the system's look and changes when the system does | A Mac app has no page head or stored choice; the system setting is the person's choice. |
| Sessions page | Pages and Behavior, One list per page; a row opens its item on its own page | The selected session shows in a side panel beside the list | The guide allows a side panel on desktop; the person keeps the inbox in view while acting on one session. |
| What's new page | Look, Color rules (status is a soft wash with a dot); Pages and Behavior, a page for reading | Each note's New or Better tag is a small blue wash chip with no dot; no cards and no tinted boxes, only a heading line per update; a picture opens bigger over the page | The tag is a label, not a status, and a dot on every note would be noise. The page is for reading, so it has no main action; a picture shown bigger floats, like a dialog, and closes with a click or Escape. |
| Archived page | Pages and Behavior, One list per page (a row opens its item on its own page) and A long list is grouped by level, with indentation | As on the Sessions page, the selected session shows in a side panel beside the list, and the list is grouped by day under plain headings with their counts, not indented | The person scans for one session and acts on it with the list still in view. A day has one level, so the heading and the row's time column are enough; it matches the Sessions page beside it. |
| Usage page (the window) and Usage by session (phone) | Look, Color rules in apps (an app's own meaning colors stay few, with a legend; never green for a data meaning) | A session's own use is the brand's blue (`--info-mark`); its agents' use is a tint of it: the info mark mixed 60% with the card ground in OKLab (`color-mix(in oklab, var(--info-mark) 60%, var(--card))` on the phone, the same values in the window), with a legend. The model bars are steel, one series each | The two parts of one session read as one family, and the tint keeps them apart in light and in dark (checked as a two-step ramp: one hue, a visible step, the light end clear of the ground). Green stays the main action and "you are here". |
| Phone pages (`phone/`) | Pages and Behavior, Pages and navigation (a section's pages are a row of tabs under the top bar) | On a phone, Sessions, Archived and Usage are tabs in a bar at the foot of the screen, each with its icon; on a desktop they are the guide's tabs under the top bar | The phone is used with one hand, often while walking, and the thumb reaches the foot of the screen, not the top. |
| Phone pages, session rows | Pages and Behavior, Pages and navigation (one list per page; a row opens its item on its own page) | Each session row also has a ↗ button at its right edge, named Open in Claude, that opens the session in the Claude app; a tap on the rest of the row opens the session's own page | Opening a session in Claude is the most common job on the phone: one tap instead of two. |
| Phone pages, Usage by session | Pages and Behavior, How a Sttark app behaves (every explanation opens from an ⓘ) | The note that a share is of your use, not a percent of your limit, shows always above the list, not behind an ⓘ | The bars sit under the limit bars and look like them: a person who misses the note reads 28% as 28% of the limit. The owner chose it in the clickable prototype. |
| Phone pages, Usage by session | Pages and Behavior, Pages and navigation (a row opens its item on its own page) | A row opens nothing in the app; only its ↗ opens the session in Claude | The phone gets no more about a session than the row shows (name, share, agents' share, last active, agents), so there is no page to open; Open in Claude is what a person does next. |
| Phone pages, Menu | Look, Top bar (who is signed in and the light, auto and dark switch sit in the bar on a desktop and open under Menu on a phone) | Menu is its own page on a phone and a desktop: the person's Macs with Remove, Sign out everywhere, Needs you notices, Look (Light, Auto, Dark) and Sign out with who is signed in. The bar holds Search and Menu only; on the Menu page Menu reads Close | Menu holds more than a panel fits on a phone (each Mac with its state and Remove), the owner chose it in the clickable prototype, and one place on both widths is easier to find. The page check's theme rule runs on Menu (`allow` in `phone/scripts/page-check.config.mjs` for the other pages). |
