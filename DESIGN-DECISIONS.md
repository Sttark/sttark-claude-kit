# Design decisions

Where Sttark Claude Kit, a native Mac app, departs from the Sttark Brand and App Guidelines' Apps chapters, which are written for web apps. Each entry: the part, the rule, the decision and why.

| Part | Rule (chapter, section) | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The window and the What's new popup | Look, Type in apps (body 16 px, buttons 15 px Semibold) | Body 13 pt, small text 12 pt, titles 15 to 19 pt, Helvetica Neue at 400 and 600 | A Mac window at web sizes reads as oversized next to every other Mac app; these are macOS's own sizes. Nothing is under 12. |
| The window | Look, Buttons (40 px tall, ink outline) and Forms (44 px fields) | macOS's own push buttons, switches, checkboxes and pop-up menus | People know them, they follow the system's light and dark look, and they work with the keyboard and VoiceOver. The one main action, Got it in the popup, keeps the green fill. |
| The window | Look, Color rules (a chosen option in ink) | Effort levels are an ink segment; switches use the system accent color | An ink switch is near white in dark mode and can't be read; the system color is clear in both. |
| The window | Look, Top bar | No black top bar; a sidebar with Sessions, Settings and What's new | A Mac window has its own title bar; the sidebar is the native way between a window's pages, and it shows the current page with the green "you are here" wash. |
| The window | Look, Icons (Lucide) | SF Symbols | The system's own icons match the system's controls at every size. |
| The window | Look, Dark mode (a light, auto, dark switch) | It follows the system's look and changes when the system does | A Mac app has no page head or stored choice; the system setting is the person's choice. |
| Sessions page | Pages and Behavior, One list per page; a row opens its item on its own page | The selected session shows in a side panel beside the list | The guide allows a side panel on desktop; the person keeps the inbox in view while acting on one session. |
